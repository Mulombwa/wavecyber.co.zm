About Wave Cyber Security

We are a Zambian-owned company that helps organisations protect themselves from digital threats and adopt technology safely.

Who we are

We provide Cyber Security and Technology Services to businesses, government institutions and NGOs in Zambia and the region. We take time to understand how each client works before recommending controls, so that security fits the way their people do their jobs.

We run internship and mentorship programmes for young professionals, giving them hands-on experience in real environments.

Our mission

To help organisations stay resilient and keep operating in the digital age, with Cyber Security and technology solutions that fit their size and sector.

We want Cyber Security to be practical in every sector. We tailor our work for:

  • Operational technology (OT), from agriculture to manufacturing
  • Financial services
  • Agribusiness
  • Small and medium enterprises (SMEs)

Our plans for 2026 to 2030

These are goals, not services you can buy today.

  • A 24/7 Security Operations Centre

    Round-the-clock monitoring and response for clients.

  • Vendor partnerships

    Building relationships with Cyber Security vendors and organisations.

  • Security awareness

    Taking an active part in ZCAN, the Zambia Cyber Security Awareness Network.

  • Research and development

    Work on AI-assisted threat detection, threat intelligence platforms and local threat research.

  • Regional expansion

    Plans to serve clients in Malawi, Zimbabwe, Botswana and other neighbouring countries.

Standards and frameworks

We work with international standards and Zambian law, including:

  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework
  • ISO/IEC 27001
  • ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management systems)
  • CIS Controls
  • Data Protection Act, 2021 (Zambia)
  • Cyber Security Act, 2025 and Cyber Crimes Act, 2025 (Zambia)

We are a registered corporate member of the Information and Communications Technology Association of Zambia (ICTAZ) and compliant with the Zambia Public Procurement Authority (ZPPA) requirements.

Technology we work in

  • Security monitoring (SIEM)
  • Endpoint detection and response (EDR)
  • Next-generation firewalls
  • Network detection and response (NDR)
  • Microsoft Azure
  • AWS
  • Google Cloud
