## Important: CC0 covers the files, not the brands

CC0 means the *image files* can be copied freely. The logos are still **registered trademarks of
their owners**. Showing a vendor's logo on your site is allowed only within that vendor's brand
rules, and normally only if you are genuinely working with them. Before launch:

1. Keep a logo only for vendors you really work with (reseller, partner or authorised
   agreement, or written permission).
2. Check each vendor's brand or partner-programme guidelines. Some require their official
   colours, minimum clear space, or a specific partner badge instead of the plain logo.
3. Where a vendor supplies an official logo pack through its partner portal, prefer that file.

Imperva and Darktrace were supplied by Wave and converted to the same single ink colour.

Not included yet: Check Point, Sophos, HPE. They appear as plain text names. Download their official
logos from their partner or press pages, put the files in this folder, and add an `<img>` in
`public/partners.html` (copy the pattern used for Dell).

Wave's own logo (`../logo.png`) and favicon (`../favicon.svg`) are Wave Cyber Security's own
artwork. There are no photographs, stock images or generated images on this site.
