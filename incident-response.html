What to do first

These steps apply to ransomware, a compromised account, phishing, or unusual activity on your network.

  1. Disconnect the device, but leave it on

    Unplug the affected device from the network (Wi-Fi and cable) to stop the problem spreading. Do not shut it down, because that can destroy evidence.

  2. Contact us

    Tell us what you have noticed, such as strange logins, locked files, a ransom note, or emails sent from your account that you did not send.

  3. Keep the evidence

    Do not delete emails, files or logs, and do not try to clean up the system yourself. Screenshots of anything unusual are useful.

  4. Change passwords from a different, clean device

    Start with email, banking and administrator accounts, once we have confirmed which systems are safe to use.

  5. Tell the right people inside your organisation

    Let your IT lead and management know. If personal data may be involved, we can help you work out your duties under the Zambia Data Protection Act, 2021.
