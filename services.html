Services

Cyber Security and Technology Services, delivered on site, remotely, as a managed service or as a one-off project.

Cyber Security

  • Cyber Security consulting

    Advice on risk management, compliance and security architecture, guided by frameworks such as NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001 and CIS Controls.

  • Penetration testing

    Authorised, simulated attacks that find weaknesses in systems, applications and networks. Testing follows recognised methods such as the OWASP Testing Guide and PTES.

  • Blue teaming

    Blue teaming is the defending side of security: watching your systems for signs of attack and stopping them early.

  • Incident response

    Containing, investigating and recovering from cyber incidents. If you need help now, see incident response.

  • Forensic investigations

    Collecting and analysing digital evidence for legal and internal investigations.

  • Data protection, training and compliance

    Support with the Zambia Data Protection Act, 2021, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies to you, and other regulatory requirements.

  • Cyber Security board training

    Training for executives and board members on Cyber Security governance.

  • Operational technology (OT) Cyber Security

    Securing industrial systems, from farms to factories.

  • Security awareness training

    Programmes for staff at all levels, tailored to the organisation.
