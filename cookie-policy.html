Cookie policy

This site does not use cookies or similar storage. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and IndexedDB, do the same job. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

2. What this site uses

Nothing. This website does not set cookies and does not use local storage, session storage or any similar technology. It loads no analytics, advertising or social media code. Because nothing needs your consent, we do not show a cookie banner.

Like any website, our web host keeps basic server logs (such as your IP address, the page requested and the time) so that it can deliver pages and protect the service. These are not cookies. They are described in the privacy policy.

3. If this changes

If we ever add something that uses cookies, such as analytics, we will ask for your consent first, before it runs, and update this page.

4. More information

Our privacy policy explains how we handle personal data. Questions about cookies: info@wavecyber.co.zm.
